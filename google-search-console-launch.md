# Google Search Console launch steps

Kazipoa is publicly reachable at https://kazijob-fjgmdyye.manus.space/.

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in with the Google account that manages the website.
2. Choose **URL prefix** as the property type and enter `https://kazijob-fjgmdyye.manus.space`.
3. Complete Google’s ownership verification. The HTML-tag or Google Analytics method may be easiest; do not share passwords or verification tokens in chat.
4. Open **Sitemaps**, enter `sitemap.xml`, and submit it. The verified live sitemap is `https://kazijob-fjgmdyye.manus.space/sitemap.xml`.
5. Use **URL inspection** for `https://kazijob-fjgmdyye.manus.space/` and one public vacancy URL such as `/vacancies/30006`, then select **Request indexing** if Google offers it.
6. Return to Search Console after several days to review indexing, coverage, mobile usability, and any structured-data issues.

The site is already public; Search Console submission does not publish the site. It asks Google to crawl and index the public pages. Google controls the timing and may take days or longer before Kazipoa appears for searches such as “Kazipoa” or “Tanzania jobs.”
