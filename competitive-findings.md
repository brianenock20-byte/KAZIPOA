# Oby Portal comparison — verified findings

## Scope and evidence quality

The owner supplied a detailed comparison between Oby Portal and Kazipoa. Those supplied claims are treated as working assumptions, not independently verified facts. Direct access to `https://oby.obycom.co.tz` and indexed Oby pages failed DNS resolution in this environment, so no authenticated or interactive inspection was performed.

A public search result describes Oby Portal’s company directory as a place to explore trusted companies, view profiles, ratings, and latest job opportunities: [1]. Public search results also show Oby job pages with closed vacancies and company pages such as Abood Bus Service and Lindi Graphite Technology: [2] [3] [4]. A public LinkedIn profile was not usable for content verification because it returned a sign-in page rather than the profile details: [5].

A separate public Instagram result refers to KaziBora, not Oby Portal, and must not be treated as Oby evidence: [6].

## Strategic implication

The safe conclusion is that Oby visibly competes on job discovery, employer/company presentation, and vacancy publishing. The user-supplied claims about Oby’s subscription packages, growth tools, and store services should be verified directly by the owner before being used in public comparison copy.
