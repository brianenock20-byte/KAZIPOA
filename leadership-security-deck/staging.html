04 · VALIDATION RIGOR

Staging proof before production

Staging is a mirror of production logic with a complete wall between data and credentials.

Isolation Rules

DatabaseSeparate credentials; resettable synthetic test data only.
StorageIsolated bucket/prefix; no access to private production files.
OAuthStaging-specific callback and labeled test provider identities.
EmailSink mailbox or provider test mode; no real delivery to users.
