# Payment reference tile findings

The supplied reference is a 1642 × 266 landscape panel with a 6.17:1 aspect ratio, so it was read as three horizontal overlapping tiles.

Tiles 1–2 confirm a near-black/dark-charcoal full-width panel with a thin blue border, generous horizontal inset, rounded corners, and no card shadow. The left column uses a mint-green uppercase eyebrow, a large warm-light heading reading “Pay through a channel available to you.”, and muted gray explanatory copy. The copy stays on the left while the payment logos begin in a separate right-side row; the overlap between tiles confirms the row is horizontally aligned with the copy rather than stacked beneath it on desktop.

Tile 3 confirms the right side contains repeated compact dark logo tiles with thin blue outlines: Airtel, Tigo Pesa, HaloPesa with “Mobile money”, and the partially visible CRDB tile. The tiles are consistent in width and height, sit in one horizontal line, and the parent panel keeps rounded right corners with comfortable right inset. The row is intentionally wider than a narrow viewport, making horizontal overflow/scroll an appropriate mobile fallback rather than shrinking logos until unreadable.
